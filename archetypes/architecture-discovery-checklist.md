## Before the Activity

- [ ] Scope, objective, and decision are explicit.
- [ ] Required participants and decision owners are confirmed.
- [ ] Existing evidence is accessible.

## During the Activity

- [ ] Facts, assumptions, opinions, and conflicts are distinguished.
- [ ] Decisions and follow-up actions have owners.

## Evidence and Quality Checks

- [ ] Material claims cite a source.
- [ ] Missing or weak evidence is recorded.
- [ ] Quality criteria are measurable.

## Risks and Escalations

- [ ] Risks have exposure, treatment, owner, and review date.
- [ ] Escalation conditions are defined.

## Completion Gate

- [ ] Required stakeholders validated the outcome.
- [ ] Unresolved items do not invalidate the decision.
- [ ] Artifacts and next actions have durable owners.
