# Staff/Principal Interview Scenarios

Leadership-loop judgment: alignment, risk, influence, operating model, and architecture over time.

Questions only — no answers. Strong responses discuss tradeoffs, failure modes, production behavior, operational impact, cost, scaling, reliability, observability, and migration.

1. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Lead a company-wide decision to pause new microservices until ownership, observability, and deployment standards catch up.
2. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Resolve incompatible event models proposed by two senior teams for the same customer journey.
3. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Set technical strategy to reduce checkout incidents without freezing product delivery for a full quarter.
4. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Respond to executive pressure to migrate to microservices when delivery problems are mostly organizational.
5. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Turn repeated customer-impacting incidents into a reliability roadmap executives and teams both accept.
6. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Facilitate a principal-level debate between strong consistency and regional availability for a tier-1 workflow.
7. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Shape architecture principles specific enough to guide teams yet flexible enough for local tradeoffs.
8. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Decide whether to centralize platform ownership of retries, mTLS, and traffic policy in a service mesh.
9. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Address a culture where teams optimize service SLAs but ignore end-to-end journey SLOs.
10. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Review a technically impressive proposal whose operational cost and cognitive load are too high.
11. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Create accountability when an incident spans gateway, service, database, network, and vendor layers.
12. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Mentor a team that keeps adding services to solve code ownership problems.
13. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Handle a critical migration where technical rollback is possible but business rollback is politically unacceptable.
14. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Make architecture reviews valuable without turning them into approval theater.
15. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Create a service maturity model that improves production readiness without becoming checkbox exercise.
16. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Decide which reliability investments are strategic platform work versus local service fixes.
17. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Lead post-incident learning when immediate cause is human error but the system made error easy.
18. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Balance cloud cost reduction with reliability commitments during an executive cost-cutting cycle.
19. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Guide teams from shared libraries to contract-first integration without duplicating work everywhere.
20. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Choose success metrics for an architecture transformation beyond number of services migrated.
21. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Build alignment when platform and product disagree on whether to fund multi-region active-active.
22. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Influence teams to adopt SLO-based release gates without being seen as delivery police.
23. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Define when architecture standards should be enforced vs waived for regulated customer deadlines.
24. **Staff Engineer · Hard** — Coach a tech lead whose service boundaries mirror Jira epics instead of business capabilities.
25. **Principal Architect · Hard** — Navigate a reorg that splits ownership of a critical customer journey across four new managers.
