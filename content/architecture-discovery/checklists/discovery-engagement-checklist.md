Use this checklist before substantive discovery begins. Mark each item **ready**, **conditional**, **not ready**, or **not applicable**, with evidence and an owner. A conditional item must state its consequence and resolution gate.

## Decision and Outcome

- [ ] The architectural decision or question is stated precisely.
- [ ] Accountable decision authority and deadline are named.
- [ ] Business outcome, baseline, target measure, and benefits owner are identified.
- [ ] Consequence of delay or no action is understood.
- [ ] Reversible and irreversible commitments are distinguished.

## Scope and Constraints

- [ ] Included capabilities, actors, processes, systems, data, regions, and environments are bounded.
- [ ] Exclusions have rationale, owner, and consequence.
- [ ] Dependencies and adjacent initiatives are visible.
- [ ] Obligations, constraints, standards, preferences, and assumptions are classified.
- [ ] Scope-change authority and escalation are agreed.

## Stakeholders and Rights

- [ ] Sponsor, outcome, domain, data, technology, delivery, operational, security/risk, commercial, and dependency roles are assessed.
- [ ] Frontline users and operators—not only managers—are represented.
- [ ] Decision, approval, recommendation, challenge, action, and risk-acceptance rights are explicit.
- [ ] Delegates and availability are confirmed.
- [ ] Material conflicts of interest or incentives are visible.

## Evidence Access

- [ ] Current diagrams, inventories, policies, contracts, incidents, telemetry, financials, code/deployment, and research sources are identified as relevant.
- [ ] Data access, privacy, confidentiality, and retention are agreed.
- [ ] Evidence owners and validation dates are known.
- [ ] Contradictory sources will be preserved and adjudicated.
- [ ] Missing high-consequence evidence has a validation plan.

## Workshops and Logistics

- [ ] Each session has one decision-relevant purpose and required participants.
- [ ] Pre-reads and evidence are distributed with sufficient time.
- [ ] Facilitation, recording, notation, remote/accessibility, and time-zone needs are covered.
- [ ] Question routes are tailored by stakeholder and evidence domain.
- [ ] Follow-up, playback, correction, and approval mechanics are scheduled.

## Governance

- [ ] Lifecycle stages and evidence gates are agreed.
- [ ] Finding, requirement, risk, assumption, decision, and action repositories are named.
- [ ] Stable identifiers and traceability conventions are defined.
- [ ] Review outcomes, conditions, waivers, escalation, and expiry are defined.
- [ ] Security classification and artifact access are appropriate.

## Resourcing and Feasibility

- [ ] Discovery lead, authors, analysts, and specialist capacity are committed.
- [ ] Vendor/partner participation and contractual access are arranged.
- [ ] Budget and schedule include evidence analysis and targeted experiments.
- [ ] Known business deadlines are reconciled with decision lead time.
- [ ] Stop, pause, rescope, and extension criteria are explicit.

## Exit Gate

Begin when the decision, authority, scope, critical participants, evidence access, recording method, and governance are ready—or when accountable owners explicitly accept bounded conditions. Do not begin broad workshops merely because calendars are available.
