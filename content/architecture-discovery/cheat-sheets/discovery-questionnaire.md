## Route Questions by Decision

Ask only questions capable of changing the decision. For every material answer request evidence, owner, confidence, contradiction, and consequence.

| Domain | Essential probes |
|---|---|
| Context | What decision, outcome, authority, deadline, scope, and exclusion? |
| Business | Which drivers, baselines, measures, capability gaps, and cost of delay? |
| Domain | Which language, rules, invariants, boundaries, events, and owners? |
| Functional | Which actors, journeys, states, exceptions, recovery, and acceptance? |
| Process | What actually happens, where does work wait, fail, or need control? |
| Quality | What stimulus, environment, response, measure, owner, and test? |
| Integration | What intent, contract, dependency, timing, ambiguity, and recovery? |
| Data | What meaning, authority, lineage, quality, lifecycle, and reconciliation? |
| Security | What assets, actors, trust, abuse, obligations, controls, and gaps? |
| Technology | What estate, workload, lifecycle, constraint, debt, and experiment? |
| Operations | Who owns support, change, incidents, recovery, capacity, and cost? |
| Modernization | What disposition, coexistence, wave, readiness, and retirement? |
| Decision | What options, gates, tradeoffs, risk, dissent, conditions, and triggers? |

## Follow-Up Ladder

1. What happened in a recent real example?
2. What evidence demonstrates it?
3. Does behavior vary by actor, product, region, load, or failure?
4. Who owns the fact and may decide?
5. What happens if the statement is false?
6. Which requirement, risk, option, or measure changes?

## Avoid

- asking the full library in order;
- leading with a preferred solution;
- accepting “always,” “never,” or averages without segmentation;
- recording consensus while hiding contradictions;
- collecting answers with no decision link or follow-up owner.
